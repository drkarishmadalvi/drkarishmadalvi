About the clinic

Dentistry, drafted around you

Every case starts with a plan

Before any drilling or fitting happens, we study your bite, your gums and what you actually want from your smile. That plan — not a one-size template — is what guides every filling, crown or implant we place.

One clinic, full-mouth capability

From a single tooth-coloured filling to full mouth rehabilitation, Smile Architect handles restorative, cosmetic and surgical dentistry in one location in NIBM, so you're not shuttled between specialists for routine care.

Real results, shown honestly

The before-and-after photos in our gallery below are our own patients, treated at this clinic — not stock photography. We think that's the clearest way to show the quality of the work.

What we do

Dental & cosmetic services

General Dentistry

Check-ups, cleaning, fillings and everyday oral care for the whole family.

Smile Makeovers

Veneers, shade correction and full smile design planned around your face.

Dental Implants

Single-tooth to full-arch implants that look, feel and function like natural teeth.

Root Canal Treatment

Single-sitting, low-discomfort RCT to save teeth that would otherwise be lost.

Crowns & Bridges

Ceramic and metal-ceramic crowns and bridges to rebuild damaged or missing teeth.

Dentures

Complete and partial dentures that restore function and a natural-looking smile.

Composite Restorations

Tooth-coloured fillings that repair decay or chips while matching your natural shade.

Teeth Whitening

Safe, in-clinic whitening to lift stains and brighten your smile several shades.
