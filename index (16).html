About the clinic

Dentistry, drafted around you

Every case starts with a plan

Before any drilling or fitting happens, we study your bite, your gums and what you actually want from your smile. That plan — not a one-size template — is what guides every filling, crown or implant we place.

One clinic, full-mouth capability

From a single tooth-coloured filling to full mouth rehabilitation, Smile Architect handles restorative, cosmetic and surgical dentistry in one location in NIBM, so you're not shuttled between specialists for routine care.
