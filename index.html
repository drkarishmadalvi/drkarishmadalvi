About the clinic

Dentistry, drafted around you

Every case starts with a plan

Before any drilling or fitting happens, we study your bite, your gums and what you actually want from your smile. That plan — not a one-size template — is what guides every filling, crown or implant we place.

One clinic, full-mouth capability

From a single tooth-coloured filling to a complete upper-and-lower denture, Smile Architect handles restorative, cosmetic and surgical dentistry in one location in Kondhwa Khurd, so you're not shuttled between specialists for routine care.

What we treat

Dental & cosmetic services

A full blueprint of care — from everyday check-ups to complete smile makeovers.

General Dentistry

Check-ups, cleaning, fillings and everyday oral care for the whole family.

Smile Makeovers

Veneers, shade correction and full smile design planned around your face.

Dental Implants

Single-tooth to full-arch implants that look, feel and function like natural teeth.

Root Canal Treatment

Teeth Whitening

Safe, in-clinic whitening to lift stains and brighten your smile several shades.
